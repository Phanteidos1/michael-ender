First turn · Story

How I got here

My first big cash came in 2006 in Hong Kong: a three-dollar rebuy tournament on PokerStars. A few years later I stood in the sun in Juárez, Mexico, waiting for trucks of scrap metal to load and sharing cigarettes with the workers. Then Antwerp and the family diamond business, then Brussels and events.

At thirty-four I was lying on my couch in Antwerp on a grey Wednesday, asking what I was doing with my life. That question became a book. The answer, so far, is people. Every chapter was about bringing them together, and it still is.
