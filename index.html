First turn · Story

How I got here

My first big cash came in 2006 in Hong Kong: a three-dollar rebuy tournament on PokerStars, and I was chip leader. A few years later I stood in the sun in Juárez, Mexico, waiting for trucks of scrap metal to load and sharing cigarettes with the workers. Then Antwerp and the family diamond business, then Brussels, trying to bring some soul to the party scene with festivals and a community of 200 people.

At thirty-four I was lying on my couch in Antwerp on a grey Wednesday, asking what I was doing with my life. That question became a book. The answer, so far, is people. Every chapter was about bringing them together, and it still is.
